# Capability Management Testing

The normative runtime, Gateway, Workbench, and visual acceptance inventory is
maintained in the [Capability Management validation section](spec.md#validation).
Exercise it through the Rust broad, Web, and visual CI profiles. Tests use fake
providers, OAuth servers, and keyrings by default; live authority/provider
checks require the repository live runner and its structured manifest.
